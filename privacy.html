1. About this notice

This notice is given under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia) as amended. It tells you what personal data the Phantom Awards website collects, why, and what your choices are. A Bahasa Malaysia version follows below. Phantom Awards is a free, fan-made voting website run by the DePhantom தமிழ் community.

2. Who is responsible (data controller)

Data controllerJeeventhiran (DePhantom தமிழ்)
Contact for privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requestsjeeven1604@gmail.com

3. Personal data we collect

  • Email address, name and profile picture from the Google account you use to sign in.
  • Your votes and predictions (which nominee you chose in each category) and the time you made them.
  • A record of your consent (the policy version you accepted and the date and time).
  • Technical data (such as IP address and browser type) that our hosting providers process automatically in their server logs.
  • Your browser's local storage keeps your sign-in session and your language, theme and sound settings. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies and we do not run analytics.

4. Why we use it (purpose)

  • To verify that each person votes only once per category (voting verification).
  • To run the awards, the predictions game and the leaderboard (shown as first name + initial after results are revealed).
  • The organizer can see which accounts voted and how many categories each completed, to prevent abuse and to pick a random voter for a giveaway. The organizer cannot see which nominee you picked in the public results, which show totals only.
  • To keep the site secure and respond to your requests.

5. Is providing your data compulsory?

Providing your data is voluntary. However, we need your Google sign-in and your consent to verify your vote, so if you do not provide it you will not be able to vote or predict.

6. Consent

We only collect your data after you actively tick the box "I agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy" on the sign-in page. We record when you agreed. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see section 10); doing so removes your votes.

7. Who we share it with

  • Google (sign-in), Supabase (database and authentication hosting) and GitHub (website hosting) process data on our behalf.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. Optional Discord alerts to the organizer contain only anonymous voter counts.

8. Storage outside Malaysia

Our providers may store or process data on servers outside Malaysia. We choose providers that apply security safeguards, and we only send them the data needed to run the site.

9. How long we keep it (retention)

Your account details, votes, predictions and consent record are kept until 90 days after the results are announced. After that they are deleted automatically. Only anonymous totals (no names or emails) are kept so results still display. You can ask us to delete your data earlier.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to ask us to correct it, to withdraw your consent, to ask us to delete it, to limit how we use it, and to ask for a copy that can be transferred. You can do this yourself:

  • Sign in, open the profile menu (your name, top right) and choose Download my data (a file with everything we hold about you), or Delete my data & account (permanent).
  • Or email jeeven1604@gmail.com. We will respond within 21 days.

11. Security and data breaches

Voting data is protected by database access rules: nobody can read other people's votes or email addresses from the public website, and only the organizer's admin account can see the voter list. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and inform affected users as the law requires.

12. Under 18

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before using this site and giving your consent.

13. Complaints and changes

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia. If we change this policy we will update the version number and ask you to accept it again.

See also the Terms of Service.
