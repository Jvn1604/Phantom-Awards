This notice is given under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia), as amended. It explains what personal data the Phantom Awards website collects, why, and what your choices are. 4. Why we use it (purpose)

  • To make sure each person votes only once per category.
  • To run the awards, the predictions game and the leaderboard.
  • To prevent cheating, spam and abuse, and to keep the site secure.
  • If the organizer announces a giveaway, to pick a random voter. Giveaway rules are announced separately.
  • To answer your requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

5. Voluntary, and your consent

Providing your data is voluntary. Without signing in and agreeing, you can still read the site, but you cannot vote or predict. We only collect your data after you actively tick the box "I agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy" (it is never pre-ticked). We record when you agreed. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (section 11). This removes your votes and predictions.

6. Who can see your data

  • Other visitors only see vote totals after the reveal, and on the leaderboard your first name and the first letter of your surname (for example "Arun K."). They never see your email or your individual votes.
  • The organizer can see the list of voters (name, email, number of categories voted, last vote time) in the admin dashboard, which is protected by a password and 2-step verification. The dashboard shows totals, not individual choices. The organizer also has technical access to the database for maintenance, but does not look up or publish how individual people voted.

9. Storage outside Malaysia

Our service providers may store or process data on servers outside Malaysia. We use established providers that apply security safeguards, and we only send them the data needed to run the site.

10. How long we keep it (retention)

  • Account details, votes, predictions and consent record: until 90 days after the results are announced, then deleted automatically.
  • Anti-spam counters: about 10 minutes.
  • Service provider server logs: according to each provider's own policy.

Only anonymous totals (no names or emails) are kept after deletion so the results can still be shown.

11. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, withdraw consent, have it deleted, limit how it is used, and receive a copy you can take elsewhere. You can do most of this yourself:

  • Sign in, open the profile menu (your picture, top right) and choose Download my data (a file with everything we hold about you) or Delete my data & account (permanent, immediate).
  • Or email jeeven1604@gmail.com. We reply within 21 days. To correct your name or picture, change it in your Google account and sign in again.

12. Security and data breaches

Access to the data is enforced by database rules, not just the website: nobody can read other people's votes or email addresses from the public site, and the admin dashboard needs a password plus 2-step verification. All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and inform affected users as the law requires.

13. Children

You must be 13 or older to use Phantom Awards (the same minimum age Google requires for an account in Malaysia). If you are 13 to 17, please ask a parent or guardian before you sign in and agree. If you believe a child under 13 has signed in, email us and we will delete the account.

14. Emails

We do not send newsletters, marketing or automatic emails, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from. We only email you if you contact us first.

15. Complaints and changes

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia. If we change this policy we update the version number at the top and ask you to accept it again before you vote. See also the Terms of Service.
