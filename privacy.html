Privacy Policy

Phantom Awards 2026 · Last updated: October 2026

Phantom Awards is a fan-made voting website run by the DePhantom தமிழ் community. This page explains what information the site uses.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. We never receive your Google password.
  • Your votes (which nominee you chose in each category) and the time you voted.

How we use it

  • To let you vote, and to allow only one vote per category for each Google account.
  • The organizer can see which accounts have voted and how many categories each account voted in. Public results show only totals, never who voted for what.
  • The organizer may pick a random voter for a giveaway.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). Sign-in is handled by Google. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep votes and account details only for the awards event and a reasonable time afterwards. To have your account and votes deleted, email jeeven1604@gmail.com.
